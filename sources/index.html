Sources

Purpose

This page documents the primary references used to define proof, verification, and auditability in on-chain systems. The focus is on verifiable claims, evidence lifecycles, and audit-ready state transitions. This page is reference-only and non-commercial.

Source hierarchy

  1. Primary sources: protocol standards and formal specifications
  2. Secondary sources: auditability and integrity frameworks
  3. Excluded sources: token issuance, marketplaces, promotional material

Curated sources

    https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/
  • Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) / RFC Editor — RFC Series
    https://www.rfc-editor.org/
  • National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) — Audit and Accountability Controls (SP 800-53)
    https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final
  • Ethereum Foundation — Ethereum Yellow Paper
    https://ethereum.github.io/yellowpaper/paper.pdf
  • OWASP — Smart Contract Top 10
    https://owasp.org/www-project-smart-contract-top-10/
